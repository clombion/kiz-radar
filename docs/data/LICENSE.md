# Licence of the festival data

`kiz.sqlite` and `site.json` adapt the festival listings of [kizomba-world.com](https://kizomba-world.com/), which
are published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are shared under the
same licence. Each event's `url` is its page on kizomba-world. The same applies to the export in `source/`.

`fares.csv` holds our own Google Flights searches and is not part of the kizomba-world data.
