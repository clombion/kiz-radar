# Licence of the festival data

`kiz.sqlite` and `site.json` adapt the festival listings of [kizomba-world.com](https://kizomba-world.com/), which are
published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are shared under the same licence.
Each event's `url` is its page on kizomba-world. The same applies to the export in `source/`.

The fares of `kiz.sqlite` (`fares`, `fare_searches`, `fare_transfers`) come from our own searches of Google Flights and
of a reseller, kept in `pipeline/fare_offers/`, and are not part of the kizomba-world data.
